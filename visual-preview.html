Three art directions

The constraint that decides this: every asset goes through AI image → Tripo3D → aggressive mesh simplification, then has to survive a 150 KB budget and a 512px texture. Fine surface detail does not make it through that pipeline. Pick the style that doesn't need it.

Economy · starting values to playtest

What an hour buys

The number that matters most is the first one: a new student must place something real inside their first session, or they don't come back. That forces starter prices low enough to clear in ~25 minutes.

Asset pipeline · paste into your own image tool

Prompts, tuned for Tripo3D

Written for the image → 3D path specifically: single object, plain background, three-quarter view, even lighting, no cast shadows. Shadows and busy backgrounds are what make image-to-3D reconstruct geometry that isn't there.
