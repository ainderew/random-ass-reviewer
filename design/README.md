## Concept prompt

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web app redesign concept
Primary request: Reimagine Aloft, a study app for a Philippine Medtech board exam student with notes converted to flashcards, spaced review, a focus timer, and an island improved using study rewards. Design a beautiful, calm, mature study workspace with an illustrated island as a meaningful visual anchor. Show the focus home screen and a compact mobile companion view. Make the interface feel welcoming and exceptionally legible, with a fresh light appearance instead of the current dark gray interface. Choose a coherent restrained palette inspired by the island. Desktop navigation: Aloft, Focus, Review, Notes, Island. Main heading 'A little focus. A little growth.' A central focus session panel with '25:00', duration choices, and 'Start focusing'. Secondary links 'Review flashcards' and 'Your notes'. Include a small island illustration and clear modest reward counters. Use whitespace, beautiful typography, subtle borders, rounded panels, and consistent iconography. Show realistic implementable UI, not a presentation slide. No fictional performance charts or readiness percentages. No device bezel. Crisp front-facing layout.

## Illustration prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: decorative wide hero illustration for Aloft study app
Primary request: A calm inviting tiny tropical Philippine island in clear turquoise water, with rounded natural rocks, lush trees and a few palms, a small simple wooden study cottage and a short wooden dock. Distant misty blue-green mountains, pale airy blue sky and soft clouds. Polished painterly 3D illustration with natural materials, soft morning light, tranquil sophisticated feel. Wide landscape composition: island in the right half, left half mostly open pale sky and pale water to allow real HTML text over it. Water and sky at left and edges fade softly to very pale icy blue #eff7f9. No letters, words, symbols, signage, people, UI, borders, or watermark. This is aspirational decorative art, not a screenshot of game progress.
