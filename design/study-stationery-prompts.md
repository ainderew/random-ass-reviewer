# Aloft card design concepts

Built-in Imagegen was used for these concepts and the decorative study-card illustration. Mockup text is illustrative and never supplies live study data. The last concept is the user's preferred structure with their requested warmer, more expressive treatment.

## Reference desk concept

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high fidelity mobile-first study app UI concept board with three straight-on iPhone-size screens, no phone frames
Primary request: Reimagine Aloft flashcards for a Philippine Medtech exam student. Completely replace the tropical island field-journal visual identity with a mature reference-desk / contemporary library catalog design. Practical, extremely clear, calm and crafted. White content sheets on cool very light gray, deep almost-black navy text, a single restrained cobalt blue action color. Precise sans-serif typography, bold legible question text, quiet horizontal dividers, minimal corners. No hero art, tropical imagery, cream/beige, italic display serif, gradients, glass, emoji, numbered decorative sections, tiny all-caps eyebrows, dashboard metrics or nested cards. Strongly differentiate real semantic sections with sentence-case visible labels: Question, Answer, Source. Generous readable text. Screen one is Review, compact navigation, progress 'Card 2 of 6', label 'Question', a large question 'What should you do before revealing an answer?', answer hidden, clear prompt 'Try recalling it first', big bottom button 'Show answer', source content hidden until reveal so it does not leak answer. Screen two same review after reveal: retains question, an Answer section clearly separated with restrained pale blue fill, answer 'Try to recall it from memory.', Source section with small document icon and 'Study guide' and exact source passage, then 'How well did you remember?' with four large thumb-friendly choices in 2x2 layout 'Again / Forgot', 'Hard / With effort', 'Good / Remembered', 'Easy / Immediately', with small next-review intervals. Screen three is setting up the card: heading 'Check this card', clear status 'Needs review', labeled editable Question and Answer fields, Source quote visibly separate and read-only, 'Subject' selector showing 'Choose a subject', a checkbox 'I checked this card against the source', and clear 'Save and approve' action. The source is visually distinct from editable fields. Consistent navigational chrome and large 44px or larger targets across all three screens. Do not add fictional medical facts, user identities, readiness scores or rewards. The concept text is illustrative only. Aim for a real app someone can understand instantly, not a marketing page.

## Study stationery concept

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity product design board, three straight-on mobile app screens side by side, no device bezels
Primary request: Reimagine the last Aloft flashcard concept with MORE LIFE AND CHARACTER. User likes its extremely clear Question / Answer / Source structure, but finds clinical blue-gray-white sterile and plain. Keep that useful structure exactly while making the app feel like a beloved independent stationery brand and a thoughtful study companion. Mobile-first for a young adult reviewing Philippine Medtech boards on iPhone/iPad. Mature, playful, literate, inviting, not juvenile and not a generic SaaS template. Visual world: warm white card stock, pale peach workspace, deep plum ink, a vivid burnt coral main button, soft apricot answer area, a subdued lilac source area. Distinctive friendly humanist sans typography, bold rounded-but-not-bubbly question text, precise readable body text, one small well-drawn abstract stack-of-index-cards or bookmark motif for personality, restrained tactile details like a slim index tab or subtle printed ruled footer. Flat sophisticated color, not gradients. Give layouts visual rhythm and character without adding chrome, confusing decoration, illustrations behind text, or giant marketing titles. Questions, answers and sources must be identifiable in ONE GLANCE using explicit sentence-case labels, spatial separation and consistent color. Screen 1 review before reveal: Aloft small wordmark, 'Review', 'Card 2 of 6', large Question 'What should you do before revealing an answer?', clear 'Try recalling it first', and accessible large coral 'Show answer' button at bottom. Screen 2 answer revealed: retain Question, Apricot Answer section 'Try to recall it from memory.', Lilac Source section 'Study guide' and quote, then four easy thumb-size recall buttons in 2x2 'Again / I forgot', 'Hard / With effort', 'Good / I remembered', 'Easy / Immediately'. Source and answer should look distinct from buttons. Screen 3 setup: 'Check this card', neutral 'Needs review' status, clearly labeled editable Question and Answer fields, visibly read-only Source passage, 'Choose a subject' selector, checkbox and 'Save and approve'. No island, tropical plants, watercolor landscape, beige journal aesthetic, italic display serif, medical stock photography, glassmorphism, exaggerated shadows, gradients, emoji, decorative badges or fictional scores. Concept copy is illustrative, not actual clinical study content. The finished feeling should be: useful study stationery with a personality, joyful enough to return to, calm enough to read for an hour.

## Decorative cards asset

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: small decorative transparent PNG illustration for Aloft flashcard study app
Primary request: a charming, finely crafted stack of three blank index cards slightly fanned, one pale apricot, one muted lilac, one warm coral, with a small deep plum paperclip. Friendly independent stationery brand style, mature and restrained, a little character from asymmetry. Soft hand-painted gouache edges with crisp flat central shapes, subtle tactile paper, gentle short natural shadow directly below. Three tiny coral drawn accent marks at top right only. Blank cards, absolutely no text, lettering, numbers, symbols, logos, faces, people, plants, islands or tropical elements. Isolated on a truly transparent background with generous clear margins, centered composition suitable for display at 88px wide. Colors harmonize with plum #38273d, coral #c64937, apricot #ffecd5, lilac #eee8f5. No full scene, no backdrop, no fake checkerboard, no glow or dramatic 3D.
