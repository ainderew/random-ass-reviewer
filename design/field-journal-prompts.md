# Aloft field journal

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool in generation mode. The mockup is a visual reference, not sample study data. The illustration is decorative, never a clinical reference.

## UI concept prompt

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity mobile-first study app design, two phone screens side by side without device bezels
Primary request: Reimagine Aloft for a Philippine Medtech board-exam student who earns an island by studying. The user explicitly rejects generic white rounded-card dashboards. Design a distinctive cohesive interface inspired by a naturalist's island field journal: tactile warm ivory paper, dark botanical green, restrained terracotta, elegant editorial typography, fine rules, numbered study tasks, small hand-painted island vignette. Mature, intimate, crafted, highly legible and practical on an iPhone. Not childish, no generic SaaS metric tiles, no gratuitous badges or repeated rounded cards. Screen 1 Today: wordmark Aloft and small real currency counters, date line, expressive heading 'Small steps. Stronger recall.' with a small island cottage illustration integrated into the page rather than a huge hero; a segmented 5 / 15 / 30 min time budget; vertically arranged numbered task itinerary 'Recall what is due', 'Revisit a mistake', each with concise supporting line. One deep-green full-width 'Begin review' button in easy thumb reach. A compact 'Your island project' strip and secondary 'Read with my notes' entry. Bottom navigation Today, Review, Notes, Island. Screen 2: a highly readable flashcard practice page, subtle progress indicator, generous question area, source reveal, and large answer/rating controls; consistent editorial styling. Show precise, implementable front-facing UI with whitespace, clear hierarchy and large touch targets. Do not show exam readiness percentages, fictional clinical claims, streak punishments, or gradient cards.

## Illustration prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: decorative watercolor vignette for a mobile study journal app, wide landscape
Primary request: a small Philippine tropical island shore with a modest wooden study cottage, palms, softly layered green mountains, calm water and small natural rocks. Hand-painted botanical field-journal watercolor with delicate pencil details, moss green, muted warm blue, hints of warm terracotta wood. Elegant and mature. Island grouped in the right two thirds with plenty of warm ivory negative space on left; soft watercolor edges disappear into a uniform warm ivory paper background #f6f3e9. Tiny but detailed scene, generous unpainted margin all around. No sky-filled rectangular scene, no photo realism, no words, text, lettering, stamps, UI, people or watermarks. Wide 3:2 composition suitable for a journal header and a small project vignette.
