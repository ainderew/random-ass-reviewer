# Urinalysis quiz reviewer

Q: Whats the preservative of choice for urine culture if it cant be plated right away?
A: Boric acid, keeps the colony count stable for 24 h

Q: First morning urine is best for what?
A: Concentrated specimen, good for protein and nitrite

Q: Normal urine specific gravity is:
A: 1.003 to 1.035

Q1. The reagent strip for protein is most sensitive to which protein
A1. Albumin. Bence Jones protein is missed by the strip

Q2. Why do you do SSA when strip protein is negative but you suspect myeloma?
A2. SSA precipitates all proteins including Bence Jones

Q3. A strip positive for blood but no RBCs on microscopy means
A3. Hemoglobinuria or myoglobinuria

Q4. Glitter cells are:
A4. Neutrophils in hypotonic urine, Brownian movement in the granules

Q5. RBC casts mean
A5. Glomerular bleeding, as in acute glomerulonephritis
